# Language Learning — Beginner to Conversational (6–12 months)

## Phase 1: Core Vocabulary and Sound System (Weeks 1–6)
The fastest path to conversational ability is vocabulary frequency. The top 1000 words of any language cover 85% of everyday speech. Learn these first, not grammar rules.

Daily template: 15 min Anki flashcard review (spaced repetition) + 20 min new input (audio or reading) + 10 min output (speaking or writing).

Week 1–2: Phonetics and survival phrases
- Learn the sound system — every phoneme in the target language
- 50 survival phrases: greetings, numbers 1–100, days, months, colors
- Start Anki deck: add 10 new cards per day, review all due cards
- Milestone: Pronounce 50 words and be understood by a native speaker (test via iTalki or HiNative)

Week 3–4: Core vocabulary acceleration
- Top 300 frequency words — Anki only, no grammar books yet
- Listen to beginner podcasts (Language Transfer, Pimsleur) for 20 min per day
- Milestone: Recognize 200+ words by ear in native audio — no translation needed

Week 5–6: Basic sentence construction
- Learn subject-verb-object patterns. 5 verb tenses maximum.
- Write 10 sentences per day about your real life (what you did, plan to do, like/dislike)
- Start shadowing: replay native audio sentence by sentence, mimic intonation
- Milestone: Write a 5-sentence paragraph about your day. Correct it with a tutor.

Common pitfalls: Spending Phase 1 studying grammar tables instead of acquiring vocabulary. Grammar rules are patterns — acquire them through exposure, not memorization.

## Phase 2: Comprehensible Input Immersion (Weeks 7–16)
Language acquisition (Krashen): You acquire language by understanding messages slightly above your current level (i+1). Comprehensible input should be 60-70% of practice time.

Week 7–10: Massive input expansion
- Daily: 30 min listening (podcasts, YouTube with subtitles in target language — NOT your native language)
- Daily: 20 min reading (graded readers, news for learners, children's books)
- Anki: continue 10 new cards per day, never skip review
- Grammar: spend 15 min studying the top 5 patterns that keep appearing in your input
- Milestone: Complete one full graded reader (Level A2) without a dictionary

Week 11–16: Active output and conversation
- Weekly: 1 italki or Tandem conversation session (30-60 min with native speaker)
- Daily: Write a 100-word journal entry. Review corrections from tutor.
- Shadowing: 15 min per day with authentic native audio
- Milestone: 30-minute uninterrupted conversation on familiar topics (hobbies, work, travel)

## Phase 3: Fluency Push (Weeks 17–26)
At this stage, acquisition happens through volume. Reduce study and increase real usage.

Daily structure: 45 min native content (TV shows, podcasts, news) + 15 min output (writing or speaking).

Week 17–20: Native content at full speed
- Watch one TV episode per day with target-language subtitles (no native language subtitles)
- Read one news article per day — look up max 5 words per article
- Start inner monologue: describe what you see and do in the target language

Week 21–26: Real-world application
- 2 conversation sessions per week on topics you care about (not just "describe your weekend")
- Write in online communities in the target language
- Milestone: Watch a 20-minute YouTube video on a topic you care about and understand 80%+

## Spaced Repetition Science
Anki algorithm spaces reviews at optimal intervals (1d → 3d → 7d → 21d → 60d...). Skipping reviews resets intervals. Even 10 min of reviews per day on a bad day is better than skipping. Never delete a card because it feels too easy — the algorithm is managing difficulty correctly.

## Common Pitfalls
- Perfectionism: Output errors are how the brain calibrates. Speak before you feel ready.
- Single-method trap: Anki + duolingo + textbook alone never produces fluency. You need comprehensible input (real content).
- Plateau at B1: Very common. The fix is more volume of authentic content, not more grammar study.
- Speaking anxiety: Fear of making mistakes is the #1 predictor of failure. Every fluent speaker made thousands of errors first.

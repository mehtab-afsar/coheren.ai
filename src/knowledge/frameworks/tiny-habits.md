# Tiny Habits
**Source:** BJ Fogg - Tiny Habits

## Core Formula

```
AFTER I [ANCHOR], I WILL [TINY BEHAVIOR], THEN I [CELEBRATE]
```

## The Fogg Behavior Model

**Behavior = Motivation × Ability × Prompt**

```
B = MAP
```

For a behavior to occur, you need:
1. **Motivation** - The desire to do it
2. **Ability** - The capability to do it
3. **Prompt** - The trigger/reminder

**Key Insight:** Don't rely on motivation. It's unreliable. Instead, make the behavior tiny (high ability) and attach it to an existing prompt.

## The Three Components

### 1. Anchor (Prompt)
An existing routine that triggers your new habit.

**Good Anchors:**
- Highly consistent (happens every day)
- Precise moment (not vague like "morning")
- Already automatic

**Anchor Examples:**
- After I brush my teeth...
- After I pour my morning coffee...
- After I sit down at my desk...
- After I put my phone on the charger...
- After I flush the toilet...

**Anchor Formula:**
"After I [EXISTING HABIT]..."

### 2. Tiny Behavior (The Habit)
The smallest possible version of the habit you want.

**How Tiny?**
- 30 seconds or less
- So easy you can do it even on your worst day
- No motivation required

**Tiny Behavior Examples:**
| Desired Habit | Tiny Version |
|---------------|--------------|
| Exercise daily | Do 2 push-ups |
| Meditate | Take 3 deep breaths |
| Read more | Read one paragraph |
| Floss | Floss one tooth |
| Journal | Write one sentence |
| Learn guitar | Pick up guitar, play one chord |
| Drink more water | Drink one sip |
| Stretch | Touch toes once |

**Why So Tiny?**
- Removes motivation barrier
- Success builds confidence
- Small starts naturally grow
- Consistency > intensity

### 3. Celebration (Reward)
Immediate positive emotion after completing the behavior.

**Why Celebrate?**
- Creates positive emotional association
- Releases dopamine
- Wires the habit into your brain
- Makes you want to repeat

**Celebration Examples:**
- Say "Yes!" or "Awesome!"
- Do a tiny fist pump
- Smile genuinely
- Tell yourself "I did it!"
- Hum a happy tune
- Give yourself a mental high-five

**The Key:** Feel genuine positive emotion. Fake celebrations don't work.

## Recipes (Complete Examples)

```
After I pour my morning coffee,
I will open my journal and write one sentence,
Then I will say "Good start!"
```

```
After I sit down at my desk,
I will write down my #1 priority for the day,
Then I will take a deep breath and smile.
```

```
After I put on my workout shoes,
I will do 2 push-ups,
Then I will say "I'm getting stronger!"
```

```
After I brush my teeth at night,
I will floss one tooth,
Then I will smile at myself in the mirror.
```

## Scaling Up (Naturally)

**Don't force growth. Let it happen naturally.**

1. Start tiny (2 push-ups)
2. After it's automatic, you'll naturally want to do more
3. On good days, do more. On bad days, stick to tiny.
4. The habit sticks because the tiny version is always achievable

**The "Pearl Habits" Concept:**
- Tiny habits are like grains of sand
- Over time, they become pearls
- Each small action layers on the previous

## The Motivation Wave

Motivation fluctuates like a wave:

```
High ───────╮      ╭───────
            │      │
            │      │
Low  ───────╰──────╯───────
         Don't rely on this!
```

**Strategy:** Design habits for low-motivation moments.
If you can do it when motivation is zero, you'll definitely do it when motivation is high.

## Troubleshooting

### "I keep forgetting"
→ Your anchor isn't strong enough. Pick something more consistent and obvious.

### "I don't feel like doing it"
→ Make it tinier. If 2 push-ups feel hard, do 1. If 1 feels hard, just get in position.

### "It feels pointless because it's so small"
→ Trust the process. You're not building a habit, you're building the *wiring* for a habit.

### "I do it but don't feel anything"
→ Celebrate more authentically. Find what genuinely makes you feel good.

## Application in CONSIST

### Onboarding Questions
- "What do you already do every morning without thinking?"
- "What's the tiniest version of your goal you could do in 30 seconds?"

### Task Design
- Default tasks to tiny versions
- Include anchor in task description
- Prompt celebration after completion

### For New Users
- Start with 1-2 tiny habits max
- Focus on consistency, not duration
- Celebrate every completion in the app

### For Struggling Users
- "Let's make this even smaller"
- "What's something you do every day we can attach this to?"
- "How can we make this impossible to fail?"

### Coaching Language
- "What's the tiniest version of this?"
- "After you [anchor], you'll [tiny habit]"
- "Celebrate! You're rewiring your brain."
- "Consistency beats intensity every time"
